What is Bitcoin SIP?

Bitcoin SIP (Systematic Investment Plan) is a disciplined investment strategy where you invest a fixed amount in Bitcoin at regular intervals, regardless of the price. This approach, also known as Dollar Cost Averaging (DCA), helps you:

  • Reduce the impact of market volatility
  • Build wealth consistently over time
  • Avoid emotional trading decisions
  • Benefit from rupee/dollar cost averaging
